# Comparative Mythology — Core Curriculum

This curriculum covers the foundational subjects required for systematic comparative study across cultures. The subjects progress from universal definitions and structural typologies, through the foundational Mediterranean and non-Mediterranean mythic corpora, to the modern theoretical frameworks that make rigorous cross-cultural analysis possible.

## Introduction to Mythology

Covers foundational definitions distinguishing myth from folklore, legend, and religion, alongside recurring cross-cultural archetypes including creation myths, flood narratives, tricksters, and heroic journeys.

[Crash Course World Mythology (CrashCourse / Mike Rugnetta)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8dPuuaLjXtNCG9Vq7vdvJytS-F-xGi7_) - Primary 41-episode video lecture series that introduces global archetypes, motifs, and comparative structures across world traditions.

[World Mythology: An Anthology of the Great Myths and Epics (Donna Rosenberg)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780844259666) - Serves as a complementary primary-source reading anthology to accompany the video lectures, providing full regional texts from Africa, the Americas, Asia, and Europe.

## Classical Mythology

Explores the ancient Greek and Roman mythological traditions in depth, covering Olympian pantheons, cosmogony, heroic cycles, and the literary transmission of myth in classical antiquity.

[Greek and Roman Mythology (University of Pennsylvania / Peter Struck)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/mythology) - Primary structured lecture course guiding learners through Greek and Roman mythic texts, religious context, and modern interpretative frameworks; audit the course for free.

[Mythology Unbound: An Online Textbook for Classical Mythology (Jessica Mellenthin & Susan O. Shapiro)](https://uen.pressbooks.pub/mythologyunbound/) - An open-access reading textbook providing deity-by-deity reference profiles and narrative overviews that complement the video lectures.

[Theogony (Hesiod / trans. H. G. Evelyn-White)](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html) - Foundational Greek primary text for cosmogony and divine succession; read this early alongside the course modules on creation myths.

[Metamorphoses (Ovid / trans. Sir Samuel Garth, John Dryden, et al.)](https://classics.mit.edu/Ovid/metam.html) - Canonical Roman epic compilation of transformation myths; read selected books (such as Books I, VIII, and XV) as complementary primary literature following the Greek texts.

## Comparative World Corpora: Ancient Near East and Northern Europe

Examines key non-classical comparative corpora through ancient Mesopotamian and Old Norse narrative sources, focusing on creation myths, divine conflicts, the heroic quest for immortality, and apocalyptic eschatology.

[Old Norse Mythology in the Sources (University of Colorado Boulder / Mathias Nordvig)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/old-norse-mythology-sources) - Structured lecture course surveying the historical and literary transmission of pre-Christian Scandinavian myths; audit the course for free alongside the primary source readings.

[The Epic of Gilgamesh (trans. Maureen Gallery Kovacs)](https://www.ancienttexts.org/library/mesopotamian/gilgamesh/) - Core ancient Near Eastern primary text on mortality, friendship, and the flood motif, providing a free full-text translation of the standard Babylonian epic.

[The Epic of Gilgamesh (trans. Andrew George)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780140449198) - An authoritative modern verse translation with comprehensive introductory analysis; read as a modern print alternative to the free Kovacs edition above.

[The Younger Edda (Snorri Sturluson / trans. Rasmus B. Anderson)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/18947) - The primary prose handbook of Old Norse mythology; read this first among the Scandinavian texts to understand the cosmological architecture and divine narratives.

[The Poetic Edda (trans. Henry Adams Bellows)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/73533) - The older poetic source collection in alliterative verse; read key poems such as Völuspá and Hávamál as a poetic complement to Snorri's prose handbook.

## Theories of Myth

Surveys major modern scholarly frameworks for interpreting and comparing myths across cultures, including psychological, structuralist, functionalist, ritualist, and archetypal approaches.

[Myth: A Very Short Introduction (Robert A. Segal)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198724704) - Core introductory textbook that systematically compares and tests all major modern theories of myth against a single test narrative.

[The Golden Bough (James George Frazer)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3623) - Historical primary text of the comparative ritualist and "dying god" school; read selected introductory chapters alongside Segal's modern critique rather than tackling the entire volume.

[Joseph Campbell and The Power of Myth with Bill Moyers (PBS)](https://www.pbs.org/show/joseph-campbell-and-the-power-of-myth-with-bill-moyers) - A six-part video interview series serving as an accessible audiovisual exploration of the monomyth and archetypal theory to complement Segal's academic analysis.
